Painting Across Lincoln County

Our Communities

Seven Towns. One County. Local Painters for All of It.

Lincoln County stretches from Lincoln City in the north to Yachats in the south, and inland through Toledo and Siletz along the river valleys. Each community has its own character, its own housing stock, and its own painting challenges. We know the differences because we live here and work in every one of these towns.

Newport

County Seat · Coastal

The hub of Lincoln County. From historic Nye Beach cottages and Bayfront commercial buildings to South Beach developments, Newport has the widest range of painting projects on the coast. Heavy salt exposure on waterfront properties demands coastal-specific products and prep.

Learn about Newport painting

Lincoln City

Vacation Rentals · Coastal

Seven miles of beach, hundreds of vacation rentals, and a mix of 1960s beach cottages alongside newer construction. Rental property owners need painting done between booking windows. We schedule around your calendar and work efficiently to minimize vacancy time.

Learn about Lincoln City painting

Depoe Bay

Oceanfront · Coastal

Home to oceanfront condos and bluff-top homes with some of the most extreme salt spray exposure on the Oregon Coast. Properties here face direct ocean weather with minimal buffer, which means more aggressive prep and more durable product choices than even other coastal towns.

Learn about Depoe Bay painting

Waldport

Residential · Bay Community

A quieter, more residential community on Alsea Bay where the Alsea River meets the ocean. Waldport has a mix of year-round residents and retirees with more affordable housing stock than the resort towns to the north. The bridge community connecting Newport to the Florence corridor.

Learn about Waldport painting

Yachats

Art Community · Coastal

The "Gem of the Oregon Coast" — a small artistic community of roughly 750 people near Cape Perpetua. Many retired and second-home owners with well-maintained properties who value careful craftsmanship and thoughtful color choices over speed.

Learn about Yachats painting

Toledo

Inland · River Town

An inland city on the Yaquina River, just 12 miles east of Newport. More protected from salt air than the coastal towns, but still very wet. Toledo has a historic downtown, residential neighborhoods, and a mill town heritage. Different prep needs than the coast — less salt, more moisture and moss.

Learn about Toledo painting

Siletz

Home Base · Inland Valley

Home base for Lincoln County Painters. Siletz sits in the river valley along the Siletz River, home to the Confederated Tribes of Siletz Indians. A rural residential community with a mix of older homes needing restoration and newer builds. Our shortest commute and our longest-standing relationships.

Learn about Siletz painting
The Route

Highway 101 and the River Roads

Our service area follows US-101 along the coast and OR-229 and US-20 inland. Lincoln County covers roughly 992 square miles of coastline, forest, and river valleys. Whether your property is on a bluff overlooking the Pacific or tucked into the trees along the Siletz River, we can get there.

We also work in smaller unincorporated communities throughout the county — Otter Rock, Gleneden Beach, Lincoln Beach, Seal Rock, South Beach, Kernville, and others. If you are in Lincoln County, you are in our service area.

Local Knowledge Matters

Coastal Properties vs. Inland Properties

Painting on the coast is different from painting inland. Even within Lincoln County, the conditions change significantly as you move away from the ocean.

Coastal (Newport, Lincoln City, Depoe Bay, Waldport, Yachats)

  • 01Salt air accelerates paint and stain breakdown — expect shorter cycles between repainting
  • 02Moisture cycling is constant — products need to handle expansion and contraction
  • 03UV exposure is intense even on overcast days — pigment and UV blockers are essential
  • 04Wind-driven rain reaches surfaces that would stay dry in calmer locations

Inland (Toledo, Siletz, River Valleys)

  • 01No salt exposure — exterior paint and stain typically last longer than on the coast
  • 02Heavy rainfall and shade create ideal conditions for moss, mildew, and algae growth
  • 03More tree cover means less UV, but more debris accumulation on surfaces
  • 04Many older homes with original siding — restoration-level prep is more common
