About Lincoln County Painters

Locally owned. Coastal expertise. Honest work on every project.

Our Story

Painting Is What We Do. The Coast Is Where We Do It.

Lincoln County Painters is a locally owned and operated painting company serving homes and businesses across Lincoln County and the Central Oregon Coast. We are not a franchise. We are not a nationwide outfit with a local phone number. We live here, work here, and stake our reputation on every project.

Our crew brings careful preparation, quality products, and clean workmanship to every job — whether it is a single bedroom in Toledo or a full exterior on the Newport waterfront. We understand the unique demands that salt air, moisture, and coastal storms place on every painted surface, and we choose our products and prep methods accordingly.

Why Lincoln County Painters

Built for the Coast

We understand the preparation and products your property needs because we live and work here year-round on the Central Oregon Coast.

Coastal Prep Expertise

Salt air and persistent moisture demand meticulous surface preparation before any paint is applied. We know what corners cannot be cut on the coast.

Licensed & Insured

Oregon CCB licensed, bonded, and fully insured for your protection. We carry the credentials that matter.

Genuinely Local

Based in Lincoln County. We know the neighborhoods, the weather patterns, and the challenges that come with painting on the Oregon Coast.

Quality-First Approach

We use coatings rated for coastal exposure and apply them over thorough prep work. The result is a finish that holds up years longer.

Our Crew

Skilled Painters, Not Subcontractors

When you hire Lincoln County Painters, our own crew shows up. We do not subcontract your project out to whoever is available. Every member of our team understands our standards for preparation, application, and cleanup.

We treat your home or business with respect — covering floors and furniture, taping carefully, and leaving your space clean when we are done. Communication is straightforward: we show up when we say we will, and we keep you informed throughout the project.
