Tips for Painting Rooms with High Ceilings

Lincoln County Painters

The Challenge of High Ceilings

Many homes on the Oregon Coast, especially those built to take advantage of ocean and forest views, feature high ceilings, vaulted ceilings, or cathedral ceilings. These architectural features create dramatic and open interior spaces, but they also present real challenges when it is time to paint.

Whether you have a great room in Depoe Bay with twenty-foot ceilings or a loft-style space in Lincoln City with an open ceiling following the roofline, here is what you need to know about painting these spaces.

Safety Comes First

The primary concern with high-ceiling painting is safety. Working above standard ladder height introduces fall risks that need to be taken seriously.

  • Scaffolding — For ceilings above twelve feet, scaffolding provides a stable, level work platform that is far safer than ladders. Professional painters set up scaffolding that spans the room, allowing them to work comfortably at height.
  • Extension ladders — For reaching high walls and trim, extension ladders positioned on flat, stable surfaces are essential. Ladders should always be set at the proper angle and secured.
  • Extension poles — For rolling paint on high ceilings, long extension poles attached to roller frames allow painters to reach from the floor or scaffolding without constantly moving equipment.

This is one of the strongest reasons to hire a professional for high-ceiling rooms. They have the right equipment and the experience to use it safely.

Color Considerations for High Ceilings

Ceiling Color

Standard height ceilings are typically painted white or off-white. With high ceilings, you have more options. Some homeowners choose to paint the ceiling a slightly darker shade than the walls to make the space feel less cavernous. Others embrace the height and keep the ceiling light to maximize the sense of openness.

Wall Colors

In rooms with high ceilings, color has a larger visual impact because there is simply more wall to see. Bold colors can be overwhelming in large, tall spaces. Neutral and mid-tone colors tend to work better, keeping the room feeling balanced rather than overwhelming or cold.

Accent Strategies

If you want color interest in a room with high ceilings, consider painting an accent wall rather than the entire room in a bold shade. The accent wall adds depth and focus while the other walls in a neutral tone keep the space feeling open.

Practical Tips for High-Ceiling Projects

Plan for Time

High-ceiling rooms take longer to paint than standard rooms. Setting up and moving scaffolding, reaching all surfaces, and applying paint at height all add time. A room that might take a day at standard height can take two to three days when ceilings are fifteen feet or higher.

Surface Preparation at Height

Cracks in high areas are common as homes settle, especially in drywall joints where walls meet vaulted ceilings. These need to be filled and sanded before painting. Cobwebs and dust accumulate on high surfaces and must be cleaned off before paint is applied.

Spraying vs. Rolling

For large, high ceiling surfaces, spraying can be faster and produce a more even finish than rolling. However, spraying requires thorough masking of floors, furniture, and any surfaces not being painted. Professional painters evaluate each space to determine the best application method.

Lighting Changes Appearance

High ceilings create complex lighting situations. The upper portions of walls and the ceiling itself may receive light differently than the lower portions. When choosing colors, view samples at different heights on the wall and at different times of day.

When to Hire a Professional

While many homeowners handle standard-height interior painting themselves, high ceilings change the equation significantly. The equipment needed, the safety concerns, and the difficulty of achieving a consistent finish at height all make professional help worthwhile.

A professional painting crew brings scaffolding, proper ladders, spraying equipment, and the experience to handle these challenging spaces efficiently and safely. Contact Lincoln County Painters if your home has high ceilings that need a fresh coat of paint.
