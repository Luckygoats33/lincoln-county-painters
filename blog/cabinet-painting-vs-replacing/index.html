Cabinet Painting vs Replacing: Cost Comparison

Lincoln County Painters

The Kitchen Upgrade Question

When your kitchen cabinets look dated, worn, or just no longer match your style, you have two main options: paint them or replace them. Both approaches can dramatically transform your kitchen, but they differ significantly in cost, timeline, and disruption to your daily life.

The Cost of Replacing Cabinets

Full cabinet replacement is a major renovation. The costs include:

  • Cabinets: Stock cabinets start around $100 to $300 per linear foot. Semi-custom cabinets run $200 to $600 per linear foot. Custom cabinets can exceed $1,000 per linear foot.
  • Demolition: Removing old cabinets and disposing of them
  • Installation: Professional installation labor
  • Countertop replacement: New cabinets often have different dimensions, requiring new countertops
  • Plumbing and electrical: Moving or adjusting connections for sinks, dishwashers, and lighting
  • Backsplash and wall repair: Walls behind removed cabinets often need repair and repainting
  • New hardware: Handles, pulls, and hinges

For a typical kitchen, full cabinet replacement commonly ranges from $10,000 to $30,000 or more, depending on the quality of cabinets chosen and the scope of related work.

The Cost of Professional Cabinet Painting

Professional cabinet painting involves removing doors and drawer fronts, thorough cleaning and preparation, priming, and applying multiple coats of durable finish. The cost is typically a fraction of replacement.

For a standard-sized kitchen, professional cabinet painting commonly ranges from $3,000 to $7,000, depending on the number of cabinets, their condition, and the complexity of the job. This includes all labor, materials, and hardware if you choose to update pulls and handles.

When Painting Makes Sense

Cabinet painting is an excellent choice when:

  • Your cabinets are structurally sound — the boxes, shelves, and drawers function properly
  • You are happy with your current kitchen layout and do not need to reconfigure cabinet positions
  • The cabinet doors are solid wood or a material that accepts paint well
  • Your countertops are in good condition and do not need replacement
  • You want a significant visual change without a major renovation
  • Budget is a primary consideration

When Replacement Makes More Sense

Cabinet replacement may be the better choice when:

  • Cabinets are physically damaged — warped, water-damaged, or falling apart
  • You want to change the kitchen layout, add cabinets, or reconfigure storage
  • Cabinets are made of thermofoil or laminate that is peeling and cannot be painted effectively
  • You are doing a complete kitchen remodel including new countertops, plumbing, and layout changes

The Timeline Difference

Professional cabinet painting takes approximately one to two weeks, during which your kitchen remains partially functional. Cabinet replacement can take three to six weeks or longer, including ordering time, and your kitchen may be completely unusable during parts of the process.

Return on Investment

Both approaches increase home value, but painting delivers a higher return relative to its cost. A beautifully painted kitchen that cost $5,000 may increase your home's appeal and value by more than that amount. A $25,000 cabinet replacement may not return the full investment at resale — though it certainly improves livability.
