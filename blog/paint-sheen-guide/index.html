Paint Sheen Guide: Flat, Eggshell, Satin, Semi-Gloss, or Gloss

Lincoln County Painters

What Is Paint Sheen?

Paint sheen refers to how much light a dried paint surface reflects. Different sheens look different, feel different, and perform differently. Choosing the right sheen for each surface in your home is just as important as choosing the right color. Here is a practical guide to help you make the best choices for your interior painting project.

Flat (Matte)

What It Looks Like

Flat paint absorbs light rather than reflecting it, creating a smooth, non-reflective surface with a velvety appearance. It hides surface imperfections better than any other sheen because there is no reflected light to highlight bumps, patches, or texture variations.

Best Uses

  • Ceilings — Flat paint is the standard for ceilings because it eliminates glare
  • Low-traffic rooms like formal dining rooms or adult bedrooms
  • Older walls with imperfections that you want to minimize

Limitations

Flat paint is the least durable sheen. It marks and scuffs easily, is difficult to clean without damaging the surface, and is not moisture resistant. Do not use flat paint in kitchens, bathrooms, hallways, or children's rooms.

Eggshell

What It Looks Like

Eggshell has a very slight sheen, similar to the surface of an actual egg. It offers a subtle warmth and depth that flat paint lacks while still being relatively forgiving of surface imperfections.

Best Uses

  • Living rooms and family rooms
  • Bedrooms
  • Dining rooms
  • Any room where you want a step up in durability from flat

Limitations

Eggshell is more washable than flat but still has limits. Heavy scrubbing can damage the finish. It is adequate for rooms with moderate traffic but not the best choice for areas that get regularly cleaned or touched.

Satin

What It Looks Like

Satin has a soft, pearl-like sheen that falls between eggshell and semi-gloss. It provides a gentle glow without being shiny. Satin is the most versatile sheen and the most popular choice for interior walls among professional painters.

Best Uses

  • Hallways and entryways
  • Kids' rooms
  • Kitchens (walls)
  • Bathrooms (larger, well-ventilated bathrooms)
  • Any high-traffic area

Why Painters Like It

Satin strikes the best balance between durability and appearance. It resists moisture reasonably well, wipes clean without damage, and looks appropriate in virtually any room. Many professional painters recommend satin as the default sheen for most interior walls.

Semi-Gloss

What It Looks Like

Semi-gloss has a noticeable shine that reflects light clearly. It creates a harder, more durable surface than lower sheens.

Best Uses

  • Trim, baseboards, and crown molding
  • Doors and window casings
  • Bathrooms, especially small bathrooms with heavy steam
  • Kitchen backsplash areas
  • Cabinets

Considerations

Semi-gloss highlights every surface imperfection. Bumps, patches, and texture variations are much more visible. Surfaces need to be properly prepared and smooth before applying semi-gloss. It also shows brush marks more readily, so application technique matters.

Gloss (High-Gloss)

What It Looks Like

Gloss paint creates a shiny, almost mirror-like surface. It is the most reflective and durable sheen available.

Best Uses

  • Front doors and entry doors
  • Cabinets (when a high-end finish is desired)
  • Accent trim and architectural details
  • Furniture

Considerations

High-gloss paint is the least forgiving of surface imperfections. Every bump, scratch, and flaw is amplified. Application requires excellent technique, and surfaces must be perfectly smooth. It is rarely used on walls.

A Practical Room-by-Room Summary

  • Ceilings — Flat
  • Walls in low-traffic rooms — Eggshell or satin
  • Walls in high-traffic rooms — Satin
  • Kitchens and bathrooms — Satin or semi-gloss
  • Trim, baseboards, and doors — Semi-gloss
  • Cabinets — Semi-gloss or gloss

Choosing the right sheen for each surface ensures durability where you need it and a beautiful finish everywhere. Lincoln County Painters can advise you on the best sheen choices for every room in your home.
